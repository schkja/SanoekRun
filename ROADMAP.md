# Roadmap

What comes next, roughly in order. Rule for everything here: fun to come back
to, never pushy, nothing for real money that a kid could buy by accident.

## Soon

- **Daily gift.** Open the game once a day and a present waits on the title
  screen: coins, sometimes an outfit piece. A streak calendar (day 1 to 7)
  gives a bigger gift on day 7. Missing a day restarts the week, nothing is
  lost.
- **Sticker album.** Small stickers drop for moments in a run (first 1 km,
  10 perfect turns, escaping the bear twice, finding a rare golden acorn).
  One page per world. A full page unlocks a frame for the share picture.
- **Funny details.**
  - The squirrel's cheeks puff up as it collects acorns and go back down
    after a jump.
  - The lumberjack shakes his fist at the bear when he escapes a close call.
  - The bear stops to sniff a picnic basket if you pass one.
  - The hawk loses a feather when it misses you.
  - Idle animation on the title: the hero yawns, the squirrel cleans its tail.
- **Pet companion.** A chipmunk (squirrel route) or a hedgehog (lumberjack
  route) that runs beside you. Unlocked with coins, levels up with you, and
  later gets a small ability (fetches a coin now and then).

## Later

- **Video replay share.** Record the last 6 seconds of the run and share it as
  a short clip, which travels much further on TikTok than a picture.
- **Weekly event world.** One special world for a week (Halloween pumpkins,
  winter lights) with its own sticker page.
- **Friends board.** Share a link with a code; everyone who opens it sees a
  board with just your group.
- **Real models and animation** for the heroes and chasers (CC0 packs:
  Kenney, Quaternius, KayKit).
- **App stores.** Wrap the web build with Capacitor for iOS and Android once
  the game has players. The web install keeps working for kids who can't
  install apps.

## Decisions to make

- **License.** Goal: others may learn from the code but not republish the
  game. Options: source-available (PolyForm Noncommercial or Business Source
  License) for the code, all rights reserved for name, logo and art.
